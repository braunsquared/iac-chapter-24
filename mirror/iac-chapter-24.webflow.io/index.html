Your aerobatics questions, answered

FAQ for aspiring aerobatic pilots

Wondering how to get started, train, or compete? Explore our most common questions and discover how you can join our welcoming aerobatics community.

How do I join the club?

Joining is simple and open to all pilots interested in aerobatics. Just reach out, attend a meeting, and we’ll walk you through every step to become part of our group.

What training is offered for beginners?

We provide mentorship, safety briefings, and hands-on practice sessions designed for newcomers. Our experienced pilots will help you build skills and confidence at your own pace.

Can new pilots enter competitions?

Definitely! Many members started with no competition experience. We’ll guide you through the basics, help you prepare, and support you at your first contest.

Which aircraft can I use to practice?

You can fly your own aerobatic-capable plane or connect with members willing to share. We’ll help you find the right fit so you can practice safely and confidently.
